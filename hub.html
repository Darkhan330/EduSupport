❓ Knowledge Base & FAQs

How to connect to the university Wi-Fi? ▼

1. Select the Edu_Wi-Fi network.
2. Enter your student account credentials.
3. If the security certificate prompts for verification, click "Trust".

What to do if I forgot my account password? ▼

You can reset it yourself using the recovery form on the main LMS page or visit the IT department (Room 304).

How to book a computer lab for group project work? ▼

Lab booking is handled through your group leader 2 days in advance via this portal (select the "Hardware" category).
